Privacy Policy for Fini Species Database

Effective date: 12 September 2026

1. About this application

Fini Species Database (“Fini” or “the App”) is operated by xPand Foundation in support of the Rai Matak reforestation program in Timor-Leste.

Fini is a bilingual English–Tetum, offline-first species database designed primarily for field technicians, farmers and managers involved in reforestation activities. It provides information about tree species, including identifying characteristics, uses, seed collection, germination guidance and practical planting tutorials.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored and protected when you use the App.

2. Information available without an account

Users may access general species information, species profiles, images and tutorial videos without creating or signing into an account.

The App does not require guest users to provide their name, email address, phone number or other direct contact information to browse this content.

3. Administrator-created accounts

Some authorised users may be given an account by an administrator to access restricted functionality. Information associated with an authorised account may include:

  • A system-generated account identifier;
  • An account name or username;
  • An assigned user or administrator role;
  • The account’s active or inactive status;
  • The authentication provider used by the account; and
  • A securely hashed password where local-password authentication is used.

Users cannot create accounts directly through the App. Account information is used only to authenticate authorised users, manage access permissions, maintain security and administer the Fini service.

Local passwords are cryptographically hashed and are not stored in readable form. Fini does not require an account holder’s email address or phone number to be stored as part of a locally managed account.

4. Information collected automatically

When the App connects to its online services, limited technical information may be processed automatically. This may include:

  • Internet Protocol address;
  • Device, operating-system or browser information;
  • Date and time of requests;
  • Requested pages, resources or application functions;
  • Login, logout and authentication events;
  • Session and synchronisation status;
  • Error, diagnostic and security information; and
  • Other standard server-log information.

This information may be used to deliver App content, maintain security, diagnose faults, monitor service availability and improve the reliability of the App.

Fini version 1.0 does not use information collected through the App for advertising or behavioural profiling.

5. Information stored on the device

To provide offline access, the App may store information locally on the user’s device, including:

  • Species records;
  • Species images;
  • Tutorial videos;
  • Selected language and application preferences;
  • Download and synchronisation information; and
  • Session or authentication information where required.

This information is stored to allow the App to continue functioning in areas with limited or unavailable internet access.

Users may remove locally stored information by using available App controls, clearing the App’s data through Android settings or uninstalling the App. Clearing App data may remove downloaded offline content and require it to be downloaded again.

6. Device permissions

Version 1.0 of the App uses internet access to retrieve and synchronise content.

The App is not intended to request access to:

  • Precise or approximate location;
  • Camera;
  • Microphone;
  • Contacts;
  • Phone or call information;
  • Notifications;
  • Photos or personal media; or
  • External device storage.

Fini version 1.0 does not include AI species identification. If future versions introduce additional features or permissions, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those features are released.

7. How information is used

Information processed through the App may be used to:

  • Provide species information and educational materials;
  • Authenticate authorised users;
  • Manage user roles and access permissions;
  • Download and synchronise offline content;
  • Maintain the App’s security and reliability;
  • Diagnose technical problems;
  • Prevent misuse or unauthorised access;
  • Support administration of the Rai Matak species database; and
  • Meet applicable legal or regulatory obligations.

Personal information will not be used for purposes unrelated to the operation of Fini or the Rai Matak program unless required by law or the individual has been appropriately notified.

8. Service providers and disclosure

xPand Foundation uses third-party technology providers to host, store, secure and deliver the App and its content. These may include Render for application hosting, Supabase for database, authentication and storage services, and Google where Google authentication is used by authorised administrators.

These providers may process information on behalf of xPand Foundation only as necessary to provide their services, maintain security, diagnose faults or comply with legal obligations.

Some providers or their systems may be located outside Australia or Timor-Leste. Limited information may therefore be processed or stored in other countries, subject to the provider’s security and privacy safeguards.

xPand Foundation does not sell users’ personal information. Fini does not contain third-party advertising.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law, necessary to protect users or the service, or required to investigate suspected misuse or security incidents.

9. Data security

Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards are used to protect information against loss, misuse, interference, unauthorised access, modification and disclosure.

These safeguards may include encrypted network connections, authenticated access, access controls and security measures provided by the App’s hosting and database providers.

No internet-connected service or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should protect their devices and account credentials and promptly report suspected unauthorised access.

10. Data retention

Personal and technical information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Operate and secure the App;
  • Maintain authorised accounts;
  • Support the Rai Matak program;
  • Diagnose technical or security issues; and
  • Meet legal, contractual or record-keeping requirements.

Account information may be deleted, deactivated or anonymised when it is no longer required, subject to legitimate security, backup and legal retention requirements.

Offline content remains on a device until it is cleared by the user, removed by the App or deleted when the App is uninstalled.

11. Access, correction and deletion requests

Authorised users may request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information associated with their account by contacting xPand Foundation.

Because accounts are created and managed by administrators, requests may need to be verified and processed by an authorised xPand Foundation administrator.

Requests can be submitted to accounts@xpand.net.au. Further information is available at https://xpand.net.au/ .

Some information may need to be retained where required for security, legal or legitimate administrative purposes.

12. Children’s privacy

Fini is designed primarily for adult field technicians, farmers, managers and reforestation personnel. It is not designed specifically for children and does not knowingly seek to collect personal information directly from children.

General educational species information may be viewed without an account. If xPand Foundation becomes aware that personal information has been collected from a child without appropriate authorisation, it will take reasonable steps to review and delete that information where required.

13. Links and external content

The App may contain links to external websites or resources. xPand Foundation is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of independently operated third-party services. Users should review the privacy information provided by those services.

14. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App, its data practices, service providers or applicable requirements change.

The revised policy will be published with an updated effective date. Material changes may also be communicated through the App or another appropriate channel.

15. Contact and complaints

Questions, privacy requests or complaints concerning Fini may be sent to:

xPand Foundation
Email: accounts@xpand.net.au
Website: https://xpand.net.au/

Please include enough information for xPand Foundation to understand and respond to the request. Privacy complaints will be reviewed and handled within a reasonable period.
